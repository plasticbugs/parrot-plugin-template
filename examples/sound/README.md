# Sound Example

Plays notes on one voice of the ColecoVision's sound chip, and a drum on its noise voice.

## Controls

- Keypad 1 to 8: play a note of the scale while held
- Stick up and down: change the octave
- Left fire: play a drum
- RESET: back to the menu
